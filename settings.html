Diagnostics & Troubleshooting

Hand Tracking Not Working?

Applies settings optimized for more lenient hand gesture detection
Forces the visual feedback panel to appear on all open tabs
Completely restarts the hand tracking system on the active tab

Common Issues:

  • Make sure your camera is enabled and working
  • Hold your hand 1-2 feet from the camera with fingers extended
  • Ensure your hand is well-lit and visible against the background
  • Try the "Optimize for Better Detection" button for more lenient tracking
  • Use "Force Show Panel" if the feedback panel isn't visible
